Due to the amendment of the Securities Transaction Tax Act enforcement decree under the South Korean government's 2025 tax reform, the transaction tax rates on domestic stocks have been revised for **transfers made on or after January 1, 2026**.

To help you manage your investment costs, we outline the changes in trading fees below. Our **Domestic Stock Tax & Fee Calculator** has already been updated to support these revised tax rates as the default option.

---

### 1. Stock Transaction Tax Rate Revisions by Market

| Market Type | Previous Rate (2025) | Revised Rate (2026~) | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **KOSPI** | 0.15% | **0.20%** | Combined: 0.05% Transaction Tax + 0.15% Special Rural Tax |
| **KOSDAQ** | 0.15% | **0.20%** | Single 0.20% Transaction Tax |
| **KONEX** | 0.10% | **0.10%** | No Change |

* **Effective Date:** Applies to stock transfers settling on or after January 1, 2026. (In practice, due to the T+2 settlement cycle, trades executed on or after **December 29, 2025** are subject to these revised rates.)

---

### 2. Practical Trading Cost Comparison

If you sell **100 Million KRW** worth of shares on the KOSDAQ market, the difference in transaction tax is as follows:

* **Under the 2025 Rate (0.15%):**
  * Tax Amount = $100,000,000 \times 0.0015 = 150,000$ KRW
* **Under the Revised 2026 Rate (0.20%):**
  * Tax Amount = $100,000,000 \times 0.0020 = 200,000$ KRW
* **Difference:** **Additional 50,000 KRW in tax**

Additionally, brokerage commissions (usually around 0.015% for typical MTS mobile accounts) apply to both buy and sell transactions, making it crucial to accurately estimate your transaction costs.
